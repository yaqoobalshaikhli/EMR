# «العلاج يجيك» — home physiotherapy video script, made to be generated with AI

**One idea:** the hardest part of treatment is often the road to it. A person in
pain waits, postpones, and gets worse. Then the doorbell rings and the
treatment is already at the door: a physiotherapist, a woman or a man, in the
living room, going step by step with them until the first free step.

- Format: Reel, 1080×1920 (9:16), 36 s: 11 AI clips of 2–4 s, then an end card drawn in code.
- Why it should land: it opens on a pain everyone has seen in their own family
  (an older man rising from a chair, a mother with a bad back), names the real
  barrier in the first seconds (the road), turns on the brand's doorbell, and ends
  on one emotional payoff (a first free step, a lifted child) rather than a list of
  services. The film works muted: the key words are on screen.
- People: AI-generated, faces and all. They are actors, not named TabeebX staff.
  Say so in the caption or the platform label if you want to avoid any doubt.
- Staff: «أخصائية» and «أخصائي» علاج طبيعي (physiotherapists, not doctors). Both
  appear. The script never says a client can choose; it says to ask.

Voice: warm, calm, close to the microphone, neither an announcer nor a sales
voice. About 85 words in all. Brand notes say AI Iraqi-dialect voices are still weak
(گ and چ especially): a real voice, a TabeebX team member or an actor, will do
more for the impact than any model. If an AI voice is used, test it on
«الطريگ» and «بالبيت» first.

## Characters (generate these as stills first, then reuse them as references)

Style block, added to every prompt: *photorealistic, cinematic, natural skin
texture, warm natural light, shallow depth of field, 35 mm, vertical 9:16, slow
camera movement, no text, no logos, no watermark.*

- **ABU HASSAN**, 68: Iraqi man, short grey hair, neat grey moustache, light-blue
  shirt, beige trousers, a wooden cane when walking.
- **UM ALI**, 38: Iraqi woman, soft beige headscarf, long-sleeved dusty-green
  blouse, long skirt; modest home clothes.
- **THERAPIST F**, 29: Iraqi woman, plain navy scrubs top, plain navy headscarf,
  calm friendly face, small navy medical bag.
- **THERAPIST M**, 33: Iraqi man, short dark hair, light beard, plain navy scrubs,
  the same small navy bag.
- **THE GIRL**, 5: pink dress, curly dark hair (seen at a distance; never a close-up).
- **Home:** a modern Baghdad apartment or house: a rug, a tea glass on a tray, a
  window with a palm outside, warm light. No signage, no Arabic text in frame.

## Shot prompts (English works best in the generators)

Add the style block to each. Use image-to-video from the approved stills.

1. *Medium side shot of ABU HASSAN rising slowly from a worn armchair, one hand gripping the armrest, the other pressing his knee, a brief wince, soft window light. Locked camera.*
2. *View through the rear window of a moving car: slow Baghdad traffic outside, UM ALI in the back seat with one hand on her lower back, looking tired. Gentle handheld drift.*

5. *Living room. THERAPIST M kneels on the rug and gently bends ABU HASSAN's knee while he sits on the sofa, supporting his lower leg with both hands. A child watches from the other end of the sofa. Slow orbit.*
6. *A rug and a yoga mat in a bright living room, curtains half drawn. THERAPIST F gently guides UM ALI on hands and knees through a slow back stretch. Calm, private. Locked camera.*

## Getting good AI footage

- **Clips of 4–5 s, one simple action each**, 3–4 takes per shot, pick the best.
  The more the people do, the more the model invents.
- **No talking to camera and no lip-sync**: the voice-over carries it.
- **Avoid:** fast movement, close-ups of hands, complex manipulation, equipment,
  any text or signs in the frame. Keep the exercises to what is shown above: a
  seated knee bend, a slow back stretch, walking with a cane.
- **Keep the scrubs plain navy.** The logo is added in post (shots 4b and 10, where
  a back or a chest faces the camera), in code, shaded by the cloth. No generator
  can draw it.
- **Same outfit, same face, every shot**: generate from the approved stills.

## Post-production (what Claude does once the clips arrive)

Cut to the beat sheet; add the Cairo text and pills; embed the logo into the
scrubs; build the end card with the brand figure, the doorbell and the call to
action; lay the voice-over and a score in C major (drone and ticking in the first
8 s); export 1080×1920 H.264 with AAC at -14 LUFS, like the other films.

## To confirm before it posts

1. **Both genders are on the team and a client can ask for either.** The script
   says «أخصائية أو أخصائي» and «اكتب...»; it does not promise a choice. If the
   choice cannot be honoured every time, keep it that way.
2. **What the physiotherapists treat.** The script shows a knee, a back and walking
   (the same ground as the physio stories). If the team does more (stroke, children,
   sports injuries), shots 5–9 can change.
3. **The hook for paid ads.** «رجلك توجعك؟» speaks to the viewer's own body, which
   Meta's ad policy on personal attributes may reject. Organic is fine. For a boosted
   version use: «أحياناً الطريگ للعلاج... أوجع من الوجع.»
4. **AI people.** Instagram may add an «AI info» label. When real staff are ready to
   film, shots 5–10 are the ones to replace; the rest of the film stays.
5. **A doctor or physiotherapist reads it once**, mainly the exercises in 5, 6 and 7.
