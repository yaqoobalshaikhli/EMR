# «الرعاية تجيك» — home nursing video script, made to be generated with AI

Same format as the physiotherapy script (`97-physio-at-home.md`), with its own story.

**One idea:** a simple injection should not become a long road. A daughter reads a
prescription (seven days of injections) and sees seven trips to the clinic with a
father who can barely walk. Then the doorbell rings and the nurse is at the door:
a man for her father, a woman for her mother, in their own home. The payoff is not
the injection. It is the daughter's relief: her father resting in his own bed, and
her morning back.

- Format: Reel, 1080×1920 (9:16), 36 s: 12 AI clips of 2–4 s, then an end card drawn in code.
- Why it should land: the hero is the one who carries it, the caregiver. Her
  tiredness is the hook; her relief is the ending. The line «حقنة بسيطة... طريق
  طويل» says the whole problem in six words. The four services (injections, wound
  dressing, cannula, drug-sensitivity tests) come in quick vignettes, each with its
  word on screen, so the film works muted.
- People: AI-generated, faces and all. They are actors, not named TabeebX staff.
- Staff: «ممرضة» and «ممرض». The company's service sheet lists male and female
  nursing staff, and the film shows both: a nurse for the father, a nurse for the
  mother. The script says to ask; it never promises a choice.
- Nothing here replaces the doctor: the services follow the doctor's prescription,
  and the film says so on screen.

Voice: warm, close, calm: the voice of someone who has just put the phone down
after a long day. About 80 words in all. As with the physiotherapy film, a real
voice (a TabeebX nurse or an actor) will carry more than an AI Iraqi voice; if an
AI voice is used, test «الطريگ» and «بالبيت» first.

## Getting good AI footage

- **Clips of 3–5 s, one simple action each**, 3–4 takes per shot, pick the best
  and trim to the beat sheet.
- **No talking to camera and no lip-sync**: the voice-over carries it.
- **Avoid:** needle close-ups, blood, readable prescriptions or signs, fast
  movement, close-ups of hands doing fine work. Shots 5–8 stay medium or macro on
  the arm and the swab; the syringe is never the subject.
- **Keep the scrubs plain navy.** The logo is added in post (shots 4b and 10, where
  a back or a chest faces the camera), in code, shaded by the cloth. No generator
  can draw it.
- **Same faces, same outfits**, every shot: generate from the approved stills.

## Post-production (what Claude does once the clips arrive)

Cut to the beat sheet; add the Cairo text, the four service labels and the «حسب
وصفة الطبيب» line; embed the logo into the scrubs; build the end card with the
brand figure, the doorbell and the call to action; lay the voice-over and a score in
C major (drone and ticking in the first 7 s); export 1080×1920 H.264 with AAC at
-14 LUFS, like the other films.

## To confirm before it posts

1. **Both genders are on the nursing team and a client can ask for either.** The
   company's service sheet says so; the script says «ممرضة أو ممرض» and «اكتب...»
   and promises no choice.
2. **The services.** Injections, cannula, wound dressing and drug-sensitivity tests
   are the four on the sheet. Say if «حسب وصفة الطبيب» is not how every one of them
   works.
3. **A drip at home (shot 7).** Show it only if your clinical lead is comfortable
   with the film suggesting home IV therapy; otherwise swap it for a second wound
   dressing.
4. **The hook for paid ads.** «٧ أيام حقن... وكل يوم الطريگ للعيادة؟» describes the
   viewer's own family, which Meta's ad policy on personal attributes may reject.
   Organic is fine. For a boosted version use: «حقنة بسيطة... بس الطريگ طويل.»
5. **AI people.** Instagram may add an «AI info» label. When real nurses are ready
   to film, shots 4b to 10 are the ones to replace.
6. **A nurse or doctor reads it once**, mainly the details in 5 to 8: where the
   injection goes, the skin test and its wait, the drip.
