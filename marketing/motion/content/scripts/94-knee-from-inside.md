# «ركبتك... من جوّه» — 3D script (job `94-knee-prp-3d`)

Scripts 5 (PRP) and 6 (knee pain) from the old batch, joined into one 3D
journey. The old versions explain in words; this one shows the inside of the
knee, which is what «شوف بعينك» promises. One object carries the whole film,
the knee, so a viewer can follow a medical idea without prior knowledge:

**cushion → wear → why weight matters → the treatment ladder → PRP step by step → at home.**

- Format: Reel, 1080×1920, about 47 s, no faces, no names.
- Two layers of text: on-screen text carries the key word and the voice-over
  carries the detail. The film works muted, and a TabeebX doctor can record
  the voice-over later.
- Style: dark, cinematic anatomy (bone in ivory, cartilage in the brand blue,
  inflammation as orange heat), then a white studio for the team and the logo.

## Before posting

- **Medical review.** A TabeebX doctor should check every claim, then add
  their name to the end card («راجعها طبياً: د. …»), as with the heart
  carousel. The claims to check:
  - The ×4 figure: each extra kilo adds roughly 4 kilos of load on the knee
    per step when walking (Messier et al., *Arthritis & Rheumatism*, 2005).
  - PRP results are gradual and vary from case to case, as the original
    script says. Never promise a cure or a result.
- **Service claims.** PRP at home, with a specialist doctor, a sonar doctor
  and a lab specialist, is taken from the original script. Confirm it is
  live before posting.
- **Caption idea.** «ركبتك تطگ؟ شوف شديصير جوّه، وشلون نعالجها درجة درجة. كل هذا ببيتك ويا طبيب اكس.»
